# Google Business Profile: the Katameya office

Everything Escape Away needs to set up its Google Business Profile, ready to paste. The website already shows the same name, address and phone in its footer, on the contact page and in its structured data. They have to stay identical everywhere, because that is how Google, Bing and AI assistants recognise the same business.

Only Escape Away can create and verify the profile: Google needs the owner's own Google account, and verification is usually a short video. It takes about an hour, plus Google's review time.

## 1. First decide: can visitors come to the office?

Google allows an address on the profile only if customers (owners or guests) can visit the office during its opening hours.

- **Yes, visitors are welcome:** follow this guide as written. The address shows on Google Maps.
- **No, it's a working office only:** create the profile as a *service-area business*. The address stays hidden on Google but is still used for verification. The website keeps showing it, which is fine.

Showing an address that doesn't receive visitors breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.

- **No keywords in the name.** "Escape Away Hospitality: Airbnb management New Cairo" breaks Google's rules. The name is exactly as on the company profile.
- **The website link** opens the site in the visitor's language: the saved choice, then the phone's language, otherwise English. The `utm_` part makes these visits show up as "Google Business Profile" in analytics.
- **In Arabic,** the site writes the address as `قطامية ديونز، مكتب 42، القاهرة الجديدة`. Google keeps one address and translates it for Arabic users, so nothing else is needed.

## 3. Categories

- **Primary: Property management company.** This is what the office does and what owners search for. The primary category matters more than anything else on the profile.
- **Secondary**, only those that are true:
  - Vacation home rental agency
  - Interior designer (the 2D and 3D design and furnishing service)
  - Short term apartment rental agency, if Google's list offers it

Pick each one from Google's own list as you type, so the names match exactly. Don't add Hotel or Real estate agency: Escape Away doesn't run a hotel or sell property.

## 4. Business description (750 characters at most)

Google keeps one description. We suggest the Arabic one, because most people searching near the office search in Arabic. The English one is ready if the team prefers it. Google's rules: no links, no phone numbers, no "best" or "cheapest".

**Arabic (525 characters)**

> Escape Away Hospitality شركة ضيافة في القاهرة الجديدة، تحوّل الشقق والشاليهات والفيلات إلى بيوت مفروشة للإقامات القصيرة والمتوسطة، وتديرها لأصحابها. نتولى كل شيء: التصميم ثنائي وثلاثي الأبعاد، والفرش والتجهيز والتصوير، ثم النشر والتسعير والحجوزات والتواصل مع الضيوف والنظافة والصيانة وتقارير الملّاك. ندير أكثر من 400 بيت في القاهرة والشيخ زايد والساحل الشمالي والعلمين والجونة، ودعم الضيوف متاح على مدار الساعة. يطلب الضيوف إقامتهم من موقعنا أو على واتساب، ويبدأ الملّاك بتقدير لعائد وحداتهم على الموقع أو بالحديث مع الفريق.

**English (628 characters)**

> Escape Away Hospitality is a hospitality company in New Cairo that turns apartments, chalets and villas into furnished homes for short and medium stays, and runs them for their owners. We take care of everything: 2D and 3D design, furnishing, styling and photography, then listings, pricing, bookings, guest communication, housekeeping, maintenance and owner reporting. We manage 400+ homes across Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, the North Coast, El Alamein and El Gouna, and our guest support runs 24/7. Guests can request a stay on our website or on WhatsApp. Owners can start with an online estimate for their unit, or talk to the team.

Every fact here comes from the company profile.

To decide. Enter the hours the office is staffed for visitors, not the 24/7 guest support: that is a phone and WhatsApp service, and the description already says it. Once the hours are set, add them to the website too (section 11).

## 7. Service areas (optional)

If the profile is a service-area business, or the team also wants service areas shown, add places within about two hours' drive of the office, as Google asks: **New Cairo**, **Cairo** and **Sheikh Zayed City**.

The North Coast, El Alamein and El Gouna are further than that. The website's destination pages cover them. Never create a profile for a place without a staffed office there: Google treats that as a fake listing.

## 9. Photos

All photos must be Escape Away's own, never stock: Google removes stock photos and can flag the profile. JPG or PNG, at least 720 × 720 px, 10 KB to 5 MB.

| Photo | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Logo | The EA logo on a plain background, square |
| Cover | One of the best Escape Away living rooms, in daylight |
| Office outside | The building and the sign, so people can find the door (this also helps verification) |
| Office inside | The reception or meeting area |
| Team at work | With their permission |
| Homes | At least three real homes per destination, the same photos as on their listings |

Add a few new photos every month. Fresh photos keep a profile active.

## 10. Verification

Google usually asks for a short, continuous video. Plan it before you start:

1. Start outside: the street or the compound entrance, so the location is clear.
2. Show the office sign with the name Escape Away Hospitality.
3. Walk in, or open the door with the office key.
4. Show proof the business runs from here, such as branded material, business papers or a work in progress on a laptop.

Have the commercial register and tax card ready in case Google asks for documents.

## 11. After Google verifies the profile

Send these three things to whoever deploys the website:

1. **The profile's link** (Share, then Copy link). It goes in the repository variable `GBP_URL` (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GBP_URL` in `.env.production`). The contact page's map link then opens the profile, and the structured data links to it.
2. **The pin's coordinates** (right-click the pin on Google Maps, then copy the numbers). They go in `office.geo` in `src/lib/site.ts`.
3. **The opening hours.** They go in `office.hours` in `src/lib/site.ts`.

Then list the same name, address and phone on:

- **Bing Places** (it can import the Google profile in one step). Bing feeds ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot.
- **Apple Business Connect**, if it's available for Egypt.
- **Facebook**, if Escape Away has a page.

## 12. Reviews

- **Ask every guest** after checkout, with the review link (*Ask for reviews* on the profile). Ask everyone, not only happy guests: filtering who gets asked is against Google's rules.
- **No rewards** in return for a review.
- **Reply to every review** within two days, good or bad, without guest details.
- **Real reviews only on the website.** They can be added to `data/reviews.json` with the guest's permission, so listing pages show them and a rating appears in search results.

WhatsApp message to send after checkout (fill in the guest's name and the link):

> Hi {name}, thank you for staying with Escape Away. If you have a minute, a short Google review helps other guests find us: {link}. Thank you!

> أهلاً {name}، شكرًا إنك قضيت إقامتك مع Escape Away. لو عندك دقيقة، تقييم قصير على جوجل بيساعد ضيوف تانيين يلاقونا: {link}. شكرًا!

## 13. Posts

One post a month keeps the profile active. Each post needs a photo of Escape Away's own, and a link to the right page:

- A new home, linked to its listing page.
- A season opening, such as North Coast summer bookings in spring, linked to the destination page.
- Owner tips, such as preparing a unit for guests, linked to the owners page.

Google is retiring its Q&A section. The website's questions and answers (the homepage FAQ and each destination guide) do that job instead.
